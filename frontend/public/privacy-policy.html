Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

MatchMaker ("we", "our", or "us") is a sports matchmaking app that helps players organise matches. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, and a hashed password when you register.
  • Player profile — sports level, ELO rating, and match history you generate through normal use.
  • Device contacts — with your permission, we read your device's contact list so you can quickly invite people to matches. Contact data is used only within the app and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Phone numbers — used to send you match invites and notifications via WhatsApp. We do not use phone numbers for any other purpose.
  • Court booking credentials — if you connect a club membership for automated court booking, your credentials are stored encrypted and used solely to book courts on your behalf.
  • Usage data — basic analytics events (screens visited, actions taken) to help us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match you with other players and organise matches.
  • To send match invitations, confirmations, and reminders via WhatsApp.
  • To book sports courts at partner clubs on your behalf.
  • To calculate and display player ratings.
  • To improve the app through aggregated, anonymised analytics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as strictly necessary to operate the service (e.g. the WhatsApp messaging provider used to deliver notifications).

4. Contact Access

The app requests access to your device contacts (READ_CONTACTS) solely to let you find and invite people you already know. Contact data is processed on-device and is never transmitted to our servers or disclosed to any third party.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and are never readable in plain text. Court booking credentials are stored encrypted. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

7. Children's Privacy

MatchMaker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
